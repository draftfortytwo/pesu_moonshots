Big research questions, one keynote at a time.

Moonshots is PES University's invitation to step inside the work that's shaping what comes next. Every month, a professor who leads one of our research areas takes the stage to open up their lab: the questions keeping them up at night, the problems they're racing to solve, and the breakthroughs still taking shape.

It isn't a lecture. It's a look behind the curtain at research in motion, from early experiments and unexpected failures to the ideas that could change an industry. Most importantly, every session ends with an open door: real, concrete ways for students to join the work, whether that's a research assistantship, a project team, or a seat at the lab bench.

Aim high. Get involved. Take your shot.

  • ResearchThe questions a lab is chasing, and what it has found so far.
  • ProjectsLive work that students can join, build on or learn from.
  • Open positionsResearch assistant, internship and PhD openings in the lab.
  • TrendsWhere the field is heading, and what to learn to get there.

Moonshots exists to close the gap between curiosity and contribution. Some of the most important research at PES is happening a few corridors away, and we want every student and faculty member to know it's there, and that they're welcome in it.
